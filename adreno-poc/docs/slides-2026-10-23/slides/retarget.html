The arithmetic Mojo emitted, fcmp, select, fmul and maxnum, passes through untouched. Anything else, barriers, shared memory, atomics, library calls, is refused rather than guessed.

llc -mtriple=spirv64v1.0-unknown-unknown \

    -filetype=obj act.bc -o act.spv

spirv-val --target-env opencl2.1 act.spv

Why a script at all

Mojo's compiler refuses a SPIR-V target: "target spirv64-unknown-unknown is not supported by this build". Its NVIDIA output is plain LLVM, and Arch's LLVM 23 has the SPIR-V back-end.

One surprise

The unversioned target emitted SPIR-V 1.4, which the OpenCL validation rejected. Pinning SPIR-V 1.0 validated and ran.

scripts/nvptx-to-spirv.py with a golden-file test; loaded on the board with clCreateProgramWithIL, which rusticl supports

This is the smallest possible bridge between what Mojo can emit and what the Adreno's driver accepts. It is honest about its limits: it handles exactly the scalar subset this kernel needs and fails closed on anything else.
