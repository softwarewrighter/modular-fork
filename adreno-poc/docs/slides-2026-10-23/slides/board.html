The board

A 2 GB Arduino with a GPU nobody targets

Arduino UNO Q

  • Qualcomm Dragonwing QRB2210
  • Four Cortex-A53 cores, reported as Kryo-V2 at 2016 MHz
  • Qualcomm Adreno 702 GPU
  • 1.7 GiB usable RAM, 870 MiB swap
  • Debian 13.1 aarch64, glibc 2.41, Mesa 25.2.6

Why it is interesting

  • Mojo's GPU support lists NVIDIA, AMD and Apple. No Qualcomm, no OpenCL, no Vulkan.
  • Mesa exposes the GPU twice: Turnip for Vulkan 1.0 and rusticl for OpenCL 3.0.
  • CPU and GPU share one small memory. Every copy and every byte matters.
  • Mojo's own requirements ask for 8 GB of RAM.

Source: adreno-poc/docs/uno-q-capabilities.md, collected over ssh on 2026-10-23

The UNO Q pairs a Qualcomm QRB2210 application processor with an STM32 microcontroller. The part I care about is the Adreno 702. Mesa drives it with two open-source stacks, Turnip for Vulkan and rusticl for OpenCL. Mojo knows nothing about either. That is the whole challenge.
