The key move in the plan was to stop and read the repositories before believing the research notes. The notes proposed building a Vulkan stack from scratch. X_eTaL-gpu already lowered to an IR and emitted OpenCL C, and the board's datasheet promised OpenCL through Mesa. So the first path was the one that needed no new code.
