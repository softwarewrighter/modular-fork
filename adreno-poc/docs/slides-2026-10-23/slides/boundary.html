The boundary

What the day established, and what it did not

Established

  • X_eTaL source, lowered by its own tool, runs on the physical Adreno 702: 71 of 71 outputs match the evaluator.
  • A kernel written in ordinary Mojo runs on Apple, NVIDIA and the Adreno, 24 of 24 on each, from one source.
  • On the Adreno, both languages reach the same Freedreno instructions for the activation.
  • Every form is captured as text, and nothing was installed on the board.

Not established

  • Native Adreno support in Mojo. DeviceContext cannot see the GPU; a C bridge launches the SPIR-V.
  • Kernels beyond this scalar subset. Barriers, shared memory, atomics and math library calls are refused, not translated.
  • Any speedup. Eight elements prove execution and agreement, not throughput.
  • A Vulkan dispatch on the Adreno, or Mojo's own runtime on the A53.

Each handoff document ends with a section titled "What this does not establish"

The second bullet on the left is the headline, and the first bullet on the right is its fine print. A Mojo-authored kernel ran on the Adreno, but through a retargeter and a C bridge, not through Mojo's own GPU runtime. Native support needs a SPIR-V target in the compiler and a runtime behind DeviceContext, and both live in Modular's closed binaries.
