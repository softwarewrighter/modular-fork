Dark boxes are the language's own compiler. Teal boxes are the vendor's or Mesa's driver. The amber box is the one piece added today: a tested script that rewrites Mojo's NVIDIA IR for OpenCL, then LLVM 23's SPIR-V back-end.

X_eTaL-gpu's OpenCL road also runs on the M1 Max and NVIDIA cards; this deck shows it on the Adreno

Read the rows left to right. The X_eTaL road and the Mojo-to-Qualcomm road meet at the same place, rusticl, and from there Mesa does the same work for both: SPIR-V to NIR to the Freedreno IR3 compiler to Adreno instructions. Mojo's Apple and NVIDIA roads are Modular's, unchanged.
